Last build

v1.3 controls

  • Cash: enable and set a different current-cash amount for each campaign.
  • Juice of the Week: choose the weekly drug; its effect and normal in-game price are shown below the selector.
  • Juice: inserts the normal 14-byte player juice record with risk 0. This remains capped at the normal three-player total. Franchise-style two-copy players are mirrored automatically into both real player-state records.
  • Apply effects of TGH? independently adds +5 to one random mapped visible stat modifier. It does not add a juice record and does not require TGH to be the selected weekly drug.
  • Zero existing risk: zeros risk on already-juiced records and resets the campaign Team Risk byte.

Custom TGH uses only the nine mapped visible stat fields and skips the unresolved hidden field in the same player progression block. The saved modifier is never written above 100. Exact stock base-rating math is not yet decoded, so this is a save-field cap rather than a proven calculation of every player's final displayed rating.
